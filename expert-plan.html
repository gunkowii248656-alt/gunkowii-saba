Plan 01 • Professional Service

Expert Plan

A professional engagement model for businesses that want experienced e-commerce, Shopify, Etsy, SEO, CRO, marketing, or digital solutions delivered around an agreed scope of work.

What Is the Expert Plan?

The Expert Plan is the most straightforward way to work with me. You have a defined business need, we agree on the work required, and I provide professional expertise to execute or improve that area.

The Simple Idea

You are paying for expertise, strategy, execution, and professional delivery rather than making your compensation dependent on future sales or uncertain performance.

How It Works

The process is designed to make the scope clear before serious work begins.

01

Discuss

We discuss your business, current situation, goals, problems, and the type of help you need.

02

Define

We identify the work required and establish the scope, priorities, deliverables, timeline, and commercial terms.

03

Execute

I work on the agreed areas and provide the agreed professional deliverables.

Benefits of the Expert Plan

Clear Scope

You know what you are paying for and what work is expected before the project begins.

Professional Expertise

You are bringing in specialized knowledge instead of trying to solve every e-commerce problem alone.

Focused Execution

The engagement can concentrate on the areas that matter most to your current business objectives.

Predictable Structure

Because the commercial arrangement is agreed in advance, the project can be planned more clearly.

Advantages & Disadvantages

Advantages

  • Clear professional arrangement
  • Defined scope of work
  • Direct access to expertise
  • Suitable for specific projects
  • Works well for businesses with a clear budget
  • Does not depend entirely on future sales

Things to Consider

  • Requires an agreed professional investment
  • Results can depend on factors outside the project
  • Traffic, product quality, pricing, and market demand still matter
  • Large projects may require more time and investment
  • Scope should be clearly defined before work begins

Who Should Consider This Plan?

Established Stores

Businesses that already have a store but need professional optimization, strategy, or technical improvements.

New Businesses

Businesses that want to establish a stronger digital foundation before investing heavily in traffic and marketing.

Busy Business Owners

Owners who understand what needs to be improved but do not have the time or expertise to execute everything themselves.

Businesses With Specific Problems

Businesses looking for help with a defined area such as Shopify, Etsy, SEO, CRO, marketing, or website development.

What You Should Consider First

Consideration Why It Matters
Your objective We need to know what the project is actually trying to improve.
Project scope A clear scope prevents confusion about what is included.
Available budget The investment should match the size and complexity of the work.
Timeline Some improvements require more time than others.
Business readiness Strong optimization cannot compensate for poor products, pricing, fulfillment, or weak market demand.
