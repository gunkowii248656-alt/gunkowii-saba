01 • UNDERSTAND THE MODEL

What Is the Commission Plan?

A commission-based arrangement changes how the professional engagement is structured. Instead of relying only on a fixed service fee, compensation can be connected to agreed commercial outcomes.

How the idea works

We first examine the business, store, products, current performance, traffic sources and growth opportunities.

If the project is suitable for a commission structure, we agree on the work to be performed, the performance measurement method, attribution rules and the commission arrangement before work begins.

The objective is to create an arrangement where both sides understand what is being worked on, what counts as a result and how compensation will be calculated.

Important: this is not a guaranteed-sales promise

A commission model does not mean that sales are guaranteed. E-commerce performance depends on many factors including product quality, pricing, market demand, traffic, competition, customer experience, fulfilment and the business's existing reputation.

The purpose of the model is to create aligned incentives and a performance-focused working relationship.

05 • MAKE AN INFORMED DECISION

Advantages & Disadvantages

A commission arrangement is not automatically better than a fixed professional engagement. The right choice depends on the business.

Advantages

  • Potentially reduces the amount of upfront financial commitment.
  • Creates stronger alignment around agreed commercial outcomes.
  • Encourages a focus on measurable growth opportunities.
  • Can be attractive to businesses confident in their products and market potential.
  • Can create a longer-term partnership rather than a simple task-based engagement.

Disadvantages & Risks

  • Results are not guaranteed because many factors are outside the specialist's control.
  • Attribution can become complicated if multiple marketing channels or team members influence a sale.
  • The business must be comfortable sharing relevant performance information.
  • A successful campaign may generate a larger commission than a traditional fixed-fee arrangement.
  • Poor product-market fit, weak fulfilment or operational problems can limit performance even when marketing work is strong.
06 • IS IT RIGHT FOR YOU?

Who Should Consider This Plan?

The commission model works best when the business has enough foundation and transparency for performance to be measured fairly.

Good Fit

  • Businesses with an existing Shopify or Etsy operation.
  • Stores with products that already have a clear market.
  • Businesses willing to improve the store and customer experience.
  • Owners who are comfortable with performance tracking.
  • Businesses looking for a growth-focused working relationship.
  • Businesses prepared to agree on clear commercial terms.

May Not Be the Best Fit

  • Businesses with no validated product or clear offer.
  • Stores with serious operational or fulfilment problems.
  • Businesses unwilling to share the information required for performance measurement.
  • Owners expecting guaranteed sales regardless of market conditions.
  • Projects where attribution cannot reasonably be established.
  • Businesses looking only for isolated design or technical tasks.
07 • BEFORE WE START

What Needs to Be Clear?

Clear expectations are essential for a fair performance-based relationship.

Scope of Work

What exactly will be handled? Shopify, Etsy, CRO, SEO, advertising, email marketing, product optimization or another agreed area?

Commission Structure

The percentage, calculation method and payment terms should be agreed before work begins.

Attribution

Both sides should understand which orders or outcomes qualify and how those results will be identified.

Tracking

The business should have a reliable way to review orders, traffic, campaigns or other agreed performance indicators.

Responsibilities

Store access, advertising budgets, product supply, fulfilment, customer service and other responsibilities should be defined.

Review Period

The engagement should have a clear review point so both sides can evaluate progress and decide what should happen next.

Performance Must Be Measurable

A commission model works best when both sides agree on how performance will be evaluated. The exact metrics depend on the project and should be established before implementation.

Orders Relevant purchases attributed under the agreed rules.
Revenue Commercial value generated within the agreed measurement period.
Conversion Improvements in the percentage of visitors taking desired actions.
Traffic Relevant visitors generated through agreed acquisition channels.
Customer Value Retention and repeat-purchase opportunities where applicable.
Profitability Where appropriate, commercial efficiency should also be considered.
08 • THE BIGGER PICTURE

Why the Commission Model Can Be Powerful

The biggest advantage is not simply paying after results. It is creating a working relationship where strategy and commercial objectives are connected.

From isolated tasks to shared growth objectives

A store can have attractive products but still struggle because customers cannot find them, do not understand the offer, do not trust the store, encounter friction during checkout or never return after the first purchase.

That is why the work can involve more than one area:

Traffic → User Experience → Trust → Product Presentation → Conversion → Retention

The commission model can make sense when the business wants the engagement to focus on this wider growth journey rather than one isolated task.
