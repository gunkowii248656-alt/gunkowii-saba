PROBLEMS I LOOK FOR

Small Friction Can Create Bigger Business Problems.

The goal isn't to criticize your store. The goal is to identify what can be improved and explain why it matters.

Unclear Store Structure Customers struggle to understand where to go or how products are organized.
Weak Product Presentation Product information may not answer the questions customers need answered before buying.
Trust Gaps Important reassurance such as policies, contact information or credibility signals may be difficult to find.
Mobile Friction A store can look acceptable on desktop while still creating problems for mobile shoppers.
SEO Opportunities Products, collections, content and internal linking may not be working together effectively.
Conversion Barriers Visitors may arrive but encounter uncertainty before taking the next step.
Marketing Disconnect Paid or organic traffic can become less effective when the store experience is not ready for it.
Retention Gaps Existing visitors and customers may not have clear opportunities to return and engage again.
