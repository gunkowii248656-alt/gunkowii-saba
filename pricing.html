02 • COMPARE

Which Plan Makes Sense For You?

The best plan depends on your budget structure, project scope, risk tolerance and preferred way of working.

Plan Best For Main Advantage Main Consideration
Expert Plan Businesses that want professional, clearly scoped e-commerce or digital work. Clear delivery structure and professional execution. Requires an agreed professional investment.
Commission Plan Businesses comfortable connecting compensation to agreed performance outcomes. Can reduce the traditional upfront commitment. Results and compensation depend on clearly defined measurement and attribution.
Trial Plan Businesses that want to test the working relationship before going deeper. Lower-risk way to evaluate the process and quality. The scope and timeframe are intentionally limited.
Reward Plan Businesses that want incentives connected to agreed achievements or milestones. Rewards can align additional compensation with measurable achievements. Targets, measurement and attribution must be agreed clearly before work begins.
03 • BEFORE YOU CHOOSE

Four Things To Think About

Choosing the right engagement model is not simply about finding the cheapest option. It is about finding the structure that makes sense for the business and the work involved.

Scope

Consider how much work is required and whether the project needs a defined professional scope or a smaller test.

Risk

Decide how much upfront commitment you are comfortable making and whether a trial or performance structure is more appropriate.

Measurement

Performance-based models require measurable objectives, reliable tracking and clear attribution.

Commitment

The right model should match the seriousness of the project, the resources available and the desired relationship.
