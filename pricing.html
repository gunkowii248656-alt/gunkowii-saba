The right working arrangement starts with understanding the actual business problem. Explore the services first, then choose the structure that makes the most sense for the project.

01 — EXPERT PLAN

Professional Work

Pay for expertise and execution.

This is the straightforward option. You have a specific e-commerce, Shopify, Etsy, marketing, SEO, CRO or website problem and want an experienced specialist to handle the agreed work.

  • Shopify & e-commerce optimization
  • Etsy store improvement
  • SEO and search visibility
  • CRO and customer journey analysis
  • Product and collection optimization
  • Digital marketing support
  • Email marketing and retention
  • Website management and development

02 — COMMISSION-BASED PLAN

Performance Partnership

Compensation connected to agreed results.

This structure can work when both sides want the relationship to have a stronger performance component. Responsibilities, tracking methods and commission arrangements are agreed before work starts.

  • Defined project responsibilities
  • Agreed performance measurement
  • Order or revenue tracking where appropriate
  • Store optimization
  • Marketing and acquisition support
  • CRO improvements
  • Ongoing performance review
  • Clear reporting expectations
Important: Results depend on traffic, pricing, products, market demand and business operations. No specific sales outcome is guaranteed.
Discuss a Partnership
03 — TRIAL PLAN

Start Small

Test the working relationship before going bigger.

If you are not ready to commit to a larger project, we can begin with a clearly defined smaller task. This gives both sides an opportunity to evaluate communication, quality and working style.

  • Small defined scope
  • Clear deliverables
  • Limited timeline
  • Store or website review
  • Specific optimization task
  • SEO or CRO improvement
  • Product-page work
  • Next-step recommendation
Best for: Businesses that want to see how the collaboration works before moving into a larger engagement.
Start a Trial
04 — REWARD PLAN

Incentive Arrangement

Reward stronger performance against agreed objectives.

This arrangement can be added to a broader working relationship when the client wants incentives tied to specific milestones, improvements or agreed performance objectives.

  • Defined objectives
  • Agreed performance indicators
  • Milestone-based incentives
  • Store growth initiatives
  • Marketing improvements
  • CRO objectives
  • Customer retention initiatives
  • Performance review
How it works: The reward structure is agreed in advance so both sides know what qualifies as successful performance.
