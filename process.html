How I Approach Digital Projects

From Storefront to Growth.

A Practical Process Built Around the Business.

I don't begin by changing things randomly. I first understand the business, identify where opportunities and friction exist, prioritize the work, implement improvements, and then use available data and objectives to determine what should happen next.

The Process

Understand → Analyze → Prioritize → Optimize → Measure → Grow

Every project is different, but the underlying approach remains focused on solving the right problem before trying to scale it.

01

Before making recommendations, I look at the business itself: products, customers, positioning, current digital presence, objectives and commercial priorities.
